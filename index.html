About NASSAB

Combining traditional genealogical heritage with modern software technology to preserve family records digitally.

Purpose of NASSAB

NASSAB is a digital genealogy project dedicated to preserving, organizing, and exploring family records and Shajra.

The purpose of NASSAB is to make genealogical information easier to access and understand while preserving connections between generations. The platform is designed to represent complete family relationships, including male and female descendants, marriages, divorces, remarriages, children from different marriages, and other complex family connections.

Users can explore individuals within the genealogy, discover their ancestors and descendants, and understand their relationship with other people in the recorded family tree.

NASSAB aims to combine traditional genealogical records with modern technology so that family history can be preserved digitally for future generations.

Platform Capabilities

Advanced Genealogical Features

Traditional family trees fail with complex family structures. NASSAB uses a relational graph architecture to handle real-world historical records accurately.

Equal Female & Male Representation

Unlike traditional records that obscure female branches, NASSAB maps all daughters, mothers, and female ancestors with complete historical context.

Remarriages & Step-Siblings

Accurately represents divorces, multiple marriages, half-siblings, and step-children without creating duplicate person records.

Bukhari Syed & Silsila Tracing

Dedicated engine for preserving patrilineal lineage claims (Silsila), verifying ancestral chains with historic documentation.

Relationship Calculator

Calculates nearest shared ancestors (LCA algorithm) to generate clear human-readable explanations between any two family members.

Evidence & Dispute Tracking

Every relationship can be backed by birth certificates, family manuscripts, or oral testimony with confidence scores.

Secure Digital Archiving

Cloud-synced database structure ensures historical family records remain preserved safely for future generations.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 — NASSAB Platform

NASSAB ("we", "our", or "platform") respects the sensitivity of personal and genealogical records. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is handled within our digital genealogy ecosystem.

1. Protection of Living Individuals

To safeguard privacy, exact birthdates, contact information, and personal details of living individuals are restricted and masked from public indexing unless explicit permission is provided by the record owner.

2. Genealogical Information & Public Records

NASSAB compiles family relationships, historical lineage connections, and ancestor data. Historical records for deceased individuals form part of the digital heritage archive for future generations.

3. Information Submitted via Contact Forms

When you submit record updates, documents, or inquiries to digital.creator380@gmail.com or WhatsApp (+92 340 8100868), your contact details are strictly used for verification and correspondence regarding your request.

4. Data Correction & Deletion Rights

If you are a living member represented within NASSAB and wish to correct, update, or request the redaction of your personal record, please contact us at digital.creator380@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.

Terms of Service

Effective Date: September 2026 — NASSAB Platform

By accessing NASSAB or submitting genealogical information, you agree to comply with these Terms of Service.

1. Information Accuracy & Verification

NASSAB aims to preserve family history accurately. However, genealogical records often rely on historical documents, oral traditions, and user submissions. Disputed lineage claims are marked transparently within the platform.

2. User Submissions & Conduct

Users submitting updates or documents confirm that the information provided is accurate to the best of their knowledge and does not violate third-party copyrights or privacy rights.
