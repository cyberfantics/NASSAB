What makes Nassab different

Built for real families, not simple trees.

Traditional records often hide female branches, flatten remarriages, and ignore milk-kinship. Nassab records those relationships properly, and keeps private information protected.

—

Rada'at, as its own relationship

Milk-kinship is handled distinctly from blood and marriage relationships, rather than folded into a generic tree.

—

Mahram & marriage eligibility

Mahram relationships and marriage eligibility are interpreted from the recorded kinship structure. Where evidence is missing, the answer stays undetermined — never guessed.

—

Privacy by design

Protected female names are masked, and a guest mode is available, so family information is not exposed by default.

How it works

Three steps, clearly separated.

1. Record

Add family members and relationships, or submit a record update for review through the Records page.

2. Review

Updates are checked before they change a record. Disputed claims are marked transparently, not silently overwritten.

3. Preserve

Records are kept as a connected lineage, and lineage certificates can be issued with a SHA-256 integrity hash.

Privacy & certificates

Private by design. Verifiable, in a precise sense.

Exact birthdates, contact details, and personal details of living individuals are restricted and masked from public indexing unless the record owner gives explicit permission.

About certificates, stated plainly: a lineage certificate carries a SHA-256 hash so the document itself can be integrity-checked. Nassab is not blockchain-verified, and its certificates are not legally certified ancestry. That distinction matters, and it is kept in all Nassab wording.

Features

Features, in plain terms.

A genealogy platform for complete family relationships, including male and female descendants, marriages, divorces, remarriages, children from different marriages, and other complex connections — without duplicate records.

Rada'at kinship

Milk-kinship is recorded as its own relationship type, distinct from blood and marriage relationships.

Mahram & marriage eligibility

Interpreted from the recorded kinship structure. Where evidence is missing, the result stays undetermined, never guessed.

Privacy by design

Protected female names are masked, and guest mode is available. Family-aware permissions are enforced, not just hidden on screen.

Lineage certificates

Certificates carry a SHA-256 hash for document-integrity checking. Not blockchain-verified, not legally certified ancestry.

Equal female & male representation

Daughters, mothers, and female ancestors are mapped with complete historical context, not obscured.

Remarriages & step-relations

Divorces, multiple marriages, half-siblings, and step-children are represented accurately, without creating duplicate person records.

Relationship explanation

The nearest shared ancestor between two people is used to generate a clear, human-readable explanation of how they are related.

Evidence & dispute tracking

Relationships can be backed by birth certificates, family manuscripts, or oral testimony, with confidence noted. Disputed claims are marked transparently.

Secure digital archiving

Records are kept in a cloud-synced structure so family history remains preserved for future generations.

Record updates & verification

New records, corrections, and lineage documents can be submitted for review through the Records page, with a ticket reference.

Explore historical people

Browse recorded historical people, search by name, and filter the list, with each person shown as a read-only historical record where applicable.

Family tree, horizontal and fan views

View a lineage as a horizontal tree, or as a fan chart, and move the focus between generations without losing the recorded structure.

Person detail, marriage history & children

Open a person to see spouses, marriage history, and children, kept distinct by marriage, with the recorded status shown plainly.

Verify Chronicle

Verify a lineage certificate by PDF file for anti-tamper integrity, or by Serial ID. Certificates use SHA-256 document-integrity checking. Not blockchain-verified, not legally certified ancestry.

About Nassab

About Nassab.

NASSAB is a digital genealogy project dedicated to preserving, organising, and exploring family records and Shajra. It is designed to represent complete family relationships, including male and female descendants, marriages, divorces, remarriages, children from different marriages, and other complex connections.

Users can explore individuals within a genealogy, discover ancestors and descendants, and understand how they are related to other people in the recorded family tree. The aim is to combine traditional genealogical records with modern technology so family history can be preserved digitally for future generations.

Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 — NASSAB Platform

1. Protection of living individuals

To safeguard privacy, exact birthdates, contact information, and personal details of living individuals are restricted and masked from public indexing unless explicit permission is provided by the record owner.

2. Genealogical information & public records

NASSAB compiles family relationships, historical lineage connections, and ancestor data. Historical records for deceased individuals form part of the digital heritage archive for future generations.

3. Information submitted via contact forms

When you submit record updates, documents, or inquiries to digital.creator380@gmail.com or WhatsApp, your contact details are used strictly for verification and correspondence regarding your request.

4. Data correction & deletion rights

If you are a living member represented within NASSAB and wish to correct, update, or request the redaction of your personal record, please contact us at digital.creator380@gmail.com.

Terms of service

Terms of Service

Effective date: October 2026 — NASSAB Platform

1. Information accuracy & verification

NASSAB aims to preserve family history accurately. However, genealogical records often rely on historical documents, oral traditions, and user submissions. Disputed lineage claims are marked transparently within the platform.

2. User submissions & conduct

Users submitting updates or documents confirm that the information provided is accurate to the best of their knowledge and does not violate third-party copyrights or privacy rights.

3. Platform intellectual property

NASSAB application software, graph resolution algorithms, and visual branding are developed by CyberFantics. Public domain genealogical facts remain part of open family heritage.
