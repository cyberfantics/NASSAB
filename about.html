About

Preserving heritage, with care.

NASSAB balances family heritage, accuracy and responsible historical interpretation, traditional genealogical understanding, modern software, privacy, and long-term preservation.

Mission

NASSAB combines traditional genealogical records with modern software, so family history can be preserved digitally for future generations — without overstating what a record can prove. Relationships are recorded with context, evidence is noted where it exists, and disputed claims are marked transparently.

NASSAB is a Flutter and Firebase genealogy and family heritage platform, with a web presence for information, record updates and verification. It is in closed Google Play testing and is not publicly listed yet.

The traditional perspective is simple: a family record is a trust. Names, relationships, and the stories behind them are passed down carefully, checked against what is known, and kept for the next generation — not reshaped to look simpler than they were. NASSAB follows that approach: record what is known, mark what is disputed, and preserve the lineage as it was received.
